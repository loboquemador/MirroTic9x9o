Experimental variant on a 9×9 board made of nine 3×3 quadrants.

Mirroring: Each round, eight quadrants are secretly paired, and each pair gets a random mirror rule (x axis, y axis, or both). One hidden quadrant is unpaired: moves there are not mirrored.

Game: 30 moves, X starts. Lines of 3 or more score n × (n − 2). The highest score wins.
